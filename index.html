Currently pursuing B.Ed. · MSVM's New College of Education, Nashik

I am Suvarna Dasharath Purkar — currently a full-time B.Ed. Scholar at MSVM's New College of Education, Nashik. With a B.C.S. and M.C.A. from the University of Pune and a background of 22 years of IT teaching experience in prior career, I bring rich classroom wisdom to my B.Ed. studies.

Now fully devoted to my B.Ed. studies, I am deepening my understanding of pedagogy, curriculum design, and educational theory — connecting it to my real-world classroom experience. I have previously conducted Arduino/IoT workshops, Python courses, and ICT teacher training. Honoured with the CSI Best Teacher Award and Gunwant Shikshak Award.

I continue to create Marathi-language digital content on Cyber Safety, author research papers on startup ecosystems and e-commerce, and work to bridge the gap between technology and the community — even while studying full-time.

🔍 Analytical Reflections · विश्लेषणात्मक चिंतन — Criterion 8
💡
Reflection 1 · Teaching Practice Outcome

Student-Centered Learning in IT Classrooms

During internship, I observed that students engaged significantly more when lessons incorporated hands-on activities (Arduino assembly, Python coding exercises) rather than theory alone. Outcome: Activity-based methodology improved student participation by an estimated 40% and reduced classroom management challenges. This reinforced my belief in experiential learning as the most effective pedagogy for IT education.

Pedagogy Outcome: Improved Engagement
🔄
Reflection 2 · ICT Integration Outcome

Digital Tools Transforming Traditional Teaching

Integrating Google Workspace, digital lesson plans, and online assessments during B.Ed. practicum revealed significant gaps in teacher-readiness across Maharashtra schools. After conducting ICT training workshops, teachers reported greater confidence in using technology. Outcome: Demonstrated the multiplier effect of teacher training — one trained teacher impacts hundreds of students annually.

ICT Integration Outcome: Teacher Empowerment
🌱
Reflection 3 · Community Outreach Outcome

Cyber Safety Awareness in Marathi — Community Impact

Creating Cyber Safety content in Marathi addressed a critical gap — most digital safety resources exist only in English. After distributing infographics and videos in local communities, feedback indicated improved awareness of phishing scams and online fraud. Outcome: Reinforced the responsibility of an IT educator to extend learning beyond the classroom into the community. Language accessibility is a form of educational equity.

Community Impact Outcome: Digital Equity
📈
Reflection 4 · Professional Growth

Pursuing B.Ed. After 22 Years — A Reflective Journey

Returning to formal education after 22 years of classroom practice was humbling and transformative. B.Ed. theories gave academic language to what I had been doing intuitively. Understanding Bloom's Taxonomy, Piaget's stages, and NEP 2020 frameworks helped me articulate and refine my teaching practice. Outcome: Professional development is not a destination but a continuous journey — and modelling lifelong learning is itself a powerful lesson for students.

Professional Growth Outcome: Lifelong Learning
